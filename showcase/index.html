  • Pain point probes — 3-5 questions designed to surface their biggest problems
  • Value proposition — ties your services to their specific business goals
  • Objection handlers — AI pre-writes responses to "too expensive", "already have someone"
  • Confidence score — 0-100 rating on how strong this pitch is for this prospect
